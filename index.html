What we treat

Services, explained in plain words

Every treatment starts with an examination and a written estimate. Consultation fee: ₹XXX (sample). We will tell you what is needed, what can wait, and what your options are.

  • 01Checkup and Cleaning

    A full look at teeth and gums, followed by removal of plaque and tartar to keep gums healthy.

  • 02Teeth Whitening

    A supervised procedure that lightens surface stains. We first check if your enamel and gums are suitable.

  • 03Braces and Aligners

    Options to straighten crowded or spaced teeth for teens and adults, with monthly review visits.

  • 04Root Canal Treatment

    Removes infection from inside the tooth so you can keep your natural tooth.

  • 05Dental Implants

    A fixed replacement for a missing tooth, placed in stages after scans and a suitability assessment.

  • 06Kids' Dentistry

    Short, patient visits for checkups, cavities and habit guidance, with parents in the room.

Not sure what you need? Book a checkup. If no treatment is needed, we will say so and suggest when to return.

First visit?

What to expect

Four clear steps. You will always know what happens next and what it will cost before we begin.

  1. 1

    Book

    Call, WhatsApp or use the form below. Tell us your concern in one line so we allow the right amount of time — usually 20–30 minutes for a checkup.

  2. 2

    Consultation and X-ray, if needed

    The doctor examines teeth and gums and takes a digital X-ray only if it will change the advice. You can see the images on screen.

  3. 3

    Treatment plan explained with options

    We list what is urgent, what can wait, and the approximate fee for each option in writing. No work starts without your consent.

  4. 4

    Treatment and follow-up

    After treatment we share care instructions on paper and call or message the next day for longer procedures. Reviews are scheduled, not left open.
